[ How It Works ]

From sentence to signal.

Three steps. You write the first. The next two happen without you.

  1. 01 Prompt

    Type a rule.

    Operators type in plain English. No models to train, no code to write, no data scientists to hire.

    > tell me if a vessel approaches within 500m of the platform

    Sentinel expands the rule, picks the cameras, and starts watching the next frame.

  2. 02 Watch

    Watch every camera.

    Sentinel scores every frame from every assigned camera against your rule, in real time. Hundreds of rules can run in parallel.

    3 of 16 Online 6 Alerts 24 FPS
  3. 03 Alert

    Get the evidence.

    When a rule fires you get the frame, the observation, the confidence, and the timestamp. Decide once. Move on.

    Rule tell me if someone falls down on the ground
    Confidence 85%
