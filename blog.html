Why AI changes your competitive strategy more than you think

AI doesn't just make your team faster. It changes what competitive advantage even means.

PlanetPulse is LIVE! 🪐

Thursday Vibes continues at Stormberry.

The solar system, live in your browser. PlanetPulse is a privacy-first, client-side tool that computes real-time planetary positions for all eight planets, rendered as a 2D orbital diagram with precise ecliptic coordinates. No ephemeris API calls. No server dependency. All astronomical calculations run directly in your browser, including the edge cases: retrograde motion, conjunctions, and the slow drift of outer planets that shifts over months.

But the real headline is the workflow.

PlanetPulse went from zero to deployed in a single session as a functioning prototype to start an idea. This is a deeply iterative, human-AI collaboration where clear intent and creative judgment guide a sovereign AI agent to ship functional software at an unprecedented pace.
