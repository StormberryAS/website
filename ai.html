The Loop is the Product

Daily operations → sales pipeline against strategy → synthesis for your next strategy review → updated strategy → daily operations. Loop after loop, the strategy gets sharper because reality keeps feeding it.

Motion 1

AI in Daily Operations.

Most AI deployments stop at the chatbot. Real value comes from AI handling concrete daily tasks: reporting, documentation, data cleaning, maintenance planning, customer communication. Plus the staff training that lets your team actually use it without legal or ethical trouble.

  • AI Literacy & Article 4 Compliance

    What ChatGPT, Copilot, and Claude can and cannot do. Prompt boundaries, hallucination risks, confidentiality lines. The literacy your team needs before they use AI tools at work, and the documented training you need to satisfy Article 4 of the EU AI Act, in force since 2 February 2025.

  • Sovereign, Agentic Workflows

    Real value comes from agents: AI that receives a goal, breaks it into steps, uses tools, makes decisions, and executes with minimal human intervention. We design and deploy agentic workflows that run on your data, in your environment, behind your security perimeter.

  • The Boring Tasks Where AI Actually Pays Off

    Reporting, documentation, data sanitisation, maintenance planning, customer correspondence, knowledge-base hygiene. The work nobody wants to do but everyone needs done. AI is excellent at it, today, with the right boundaries.

Motion 2

AI on Your Sales Pipeline.

A sales pipeline is full of opportunities that don't all matter equally. AI can score every deal against your strategy and surface the ones that move you toward where you actually want to go. Less time on noise, more on the right rooms.
